## About us

StopSlop is an AI-focused engineering and consulting company. We make AI useful in everyday work and keep the systems behind it reliable. Clear workflows, less waste, and people in control.

## What we do

| Service | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| AI strategy and consulting | Decide where AI pays off, where it does not, and what to do first. |
| Workflow design | Turn individual experiments into shared processes with clear standards and human review. |
| Team enablement | Practical training and playbooks to use AI confidently and consistently. |
| AI Ops | Model and prompt lifecycle, evaluation, cost control, and monitoring for LLM-based systems. |
| DevOps and Platform Engineering | CI/CD, Kubernetes, infrastructure as code, and developer self-service. |
| Observability | Metrics, logs, traces, and alerts that lead to decisions, not noise. |

## Our approach

1. **Understand** - Choose one process. Agree on the problem and a baseline for time, cost, and quality.
2. **Test** - Run a focused pilot with your team. Compare the results with how you work today.
3. **Embed** - Keep what works. Document the process, train the team, and make ownership clear.
